Our Story

What We Stand For

Our Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the principles that guide every seed we plant and every tray we harvest.

01

Freshness Above All

We believe that food should be as close to alive as possible when it reaches your table. That's why we never pre-harvest, never store for weeks, and never compromise on freshness for convenience.

02

Clean & Conscious Growing

Our greens are grown from organic seeds, powered by renewable energy, and watered with nothing but clean water. No pesticides, no synthetic fertilizers, no shortcuts. We eat what we grow, and we grow what we'd proudly feed our own children.

03

Community First

We're not trying to ship across the country. We want to feed our neighbors in Franklin and beyond — at the Franklin Farmers Market every Saturday and through direct orders — and be a meaningful part of a more local, connected food system.

How We Grow

From Seed to Your Table

Every tray goes through the same careful process. We don't rush it, we don't skip steps, and we check on our greens every single day.

1

Seed Selection

We source certified organic seeds specifically selected for microgreen growing — high germination rates, clean origin, and superior flavor.

2

Tray Preparation

Each tray is filled with our growing medium — a clean, neutral substrate that holds moisture beautifully without harboring pathogens.

3

Germination

Seeds are evenly sown and covered to encourage even germination. We check moisture levels twice daily during this critical first phase.

4

Growing & Daily Care

Once sprouted, trays move to our light-filled growing area. We water carefully from the bottom to prevent mold and encourage strong root development.

5

Harvest to Order

When you place your order, we harvest your tray at peak nutrition and freshness — then it goes straight to you within 24 hours.
