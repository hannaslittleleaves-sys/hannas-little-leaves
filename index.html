Why Choose Us

The Hanna's Difference

We're not a warehouse operation. We're a family who genuinely loves growing food and sharing it with our neighbors.

Cut Fresh to Order

We harvest within 24 hours of your pickup or delivery. You receive peak nutrition and maximum flavor — every single time.

No Chemicals, Ever

We use only clean water and quality seeds. No pesticides, no synthetic fertilizers — just pure, natural growing the way nature intended.
